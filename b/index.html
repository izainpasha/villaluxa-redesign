Good to know

Questions guests ask

Can I arrive early or leave late?

Early check-in is €20 (room from 12:00). Late check-out is €20 (keep it until 14:00). Add either when you book.

What's the cancellation policy?

On the Flexible rate, cancel free up to 7 days before arrival; closer to the date a charge may apply. Non-refundable bookings can't be cancelled free of charge.

Is there parking?

Not at the house, and the historic centre is one-way streets. Leave the car in a nearby public car park and take a taxi or Bolt to the door.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, for €20 per stay, in every room except the Deluxe Double. Let us know in advance.

How far are the palaces?

The National Palace is 3 minutes on foot, Quinta da Regaleira 7, and Pena 15 minutes by bus.

How do I pay?

Send your request and the house confirms availability and the price on WhatsApp. Card payment online is coming soon.
