Not a hotel in the usual sense. A townhouse, kept the old way.

Villa Luxa is a small house in the historic centre of Sintra, furnished with antiques, gilt mirrors and carved consoles. There is no lobby queue. There is a wood stove in the lounge, tea and coffee in the lobby, and someone from the house on the other end of WhatsApp.

Guests tell us it feels like staying with a friend who happens to own very good furniture. We take that as the point.

Before you book

The practical things guests ask most. Anything else, the house is on WhatsApp.

Message the house
Can I arrive early or leave late?

Early check-in is €20 and gives you the room from 12:00. Late check-out is €20 and keeps it until 14:00. Add either when you book, or ask in advance.

What is the cancellation policy?

On the Flexible rate you can cancel free of charge up to 7 days before you arrive; closer to the date a charge may apply. A Non-refundable booking can't be cancelled free of charge.

Is there parking?

There's no parking in front of the house, and the historic centre is a maze of one-way streets. Use one of the nearby public car parks and take a taxi or Bolt (not Uber) to the door.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, for €20 per stay, in every room except the Deluxe Double. Let us know in advance.

Is the house suitable for families?

Both Family Rooms sleep four, with a king and a queen bed. The Family Room with Terrace has its own outdoor seating.
